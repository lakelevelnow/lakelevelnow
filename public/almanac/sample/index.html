Where these numbers come from

Every figure in this almanac is computed from one series: the daily reservoir pool elevation published by USBR for site 919 as parameter 49, in feet above the Reclamation pool datum (NGVD 1929). The station page is https://www.usbr.gov/uc/water/hydrodata/reservoir_data/919/csv/49.csv.

0 readings (0.000% of the raw series) were discarded before any statistic was computed: 0 fell outside a robust band around the record's own median, and 0 were single-day jumps larger than a reservoir surface can physically make. Nothing was smoothed, interpolated or back-filled. The discarded rows are the only difference between daily-record.csv in this download and what the agency serves.

What this almanac does not do. It reports what the water has done. It does not tell you whether a ramp is open, whether a dock floats, or whether it is safe to launch. Closures depend on elevation, but also on staffing, silt, maintenance and, in California through 2025 and 2026, invasive-mussel inspections that closed ramps at lakes which were not low. Use the elevation tool above with a threshold you have verified yourself, and confirm current conditions with the operating agency before you tow anything.
